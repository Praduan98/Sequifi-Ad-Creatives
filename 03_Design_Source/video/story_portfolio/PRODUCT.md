# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

The creator's LinkedIn network, met in a scrolling feed on phones and laptops:

- designers and motion people, who judge the craft (characters, motion, type);
- AI builders and developers, who care that the film is made in code and that the skill is open source;
- clients and recruiters, who want proof of polished brand films.

## Product Purpose

The portfolio cut of a 45 s concept brand film, and the open-source Claude Code skill that made it (motion-film). Promo
surfaces around the film (its thumbnail, the post) exist to get people to stop, watch, look at the repo and connect.

## Positioning

The film is drawn entirely in code: every frame is a function of time, rendered with real motion blur by headless Chrome,
with a synthesized score. The workflow is published as the motion-film skill.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Film: 1920 × 1080, 30 fps, 45 s; kinetic type; a 3D toy cast built in Three.js (periwinkle hero with an antenna, mint
  field rep in a hard hat, coral manager in glasses, yellow operator in a headset).
- No client branding anywhere: no client name, logo, URL or trust badges.
- motion-film is v0.1 (engine, render pipeline, QA checks). The brief → storyboard → film flow and the extra formats are
  roadmap, not shipped.

## Brand Commitments

- Headline for the promo surfaces: "Drawn entirely in code."
- Credit: the GitHub mark with "github.com/Praduan-1510"; no other name.
- The open-source indication uses the GitHub logo.

## Evidence on Hand

- The film: `04_Final_Exports/Video/Portfolio/Brand_Story_45s_Portfolio_1080p.mp4` (and `_web.mp4`).
- The skill: https://github.com/Praduan-1510/motion-film (public, MIT licence since 2026-10-26, so "open source" is accurate).
- Open decision: some on-screen figures and lines in the film come from the client's website; whether promo surfaces may
  feature them is undecided, so they stay off promo surfaces for now.

## Product Principles

- Show the craft working; let the motion prove the claim.
- Be exact about the mechanism: code, not After Effects; v0.1, not a finished product.
- The cast is the face of the work; nothing that identifies the client.
