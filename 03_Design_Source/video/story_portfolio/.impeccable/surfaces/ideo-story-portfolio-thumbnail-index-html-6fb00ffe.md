# Thumbnail: portfolio brand film

Scope: the LinkedIn video thumbnail (1920 × 1080 PNG) for the 45 s portfolio cut. Visitor mode: Persuade. An extension of
the film's established world (its palette, DM Sans type, selection-box device, cursor and 3D toy cast); no new identity.

Audience: designers and motion people, AI builders and developers, clients and recruiters, in a light LinkedIn feed.
Job: stop the scroll and get the play. Content: headline "Drawn entirely in code.", the cast, a "45s brand film" label,
an open-source tag with the GitHub mark, credit "github.com/Praduan-1510". Constraints: no client branding or client
figures; LinkedIn draws its own play button at the centre; must read at phone feed width (~390 px).

## Direction contract

THESIS: The thumbnail is an animator's spacing chart: the hero's leap drawn as an onion-skinned arc, so the motion itself
is the image. It refuses the category default, a paused frame with a big title slapped on it.

OWN-WORLD: The film's night: navy #070B22 into a periwinkle glow, ice type #EEF1FF in DM Sans with weight contrast, the film's selection box (thin periwinkle frame, square handles) and
its cursor; glossy 3D toys in periwinkle, mint, coral and yellow; time stamps set as measurement, not decoration.

STORY: A cute 3D character is caught mid-leap with its own motion study behind it; the headline says how: drawn
entirely in code. The team below watches it. The viewer gets "slick motion piece, made in code, open source" and plays.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Headline top-left at ~150 px, two lines, "code." bold inside the selection box with the cursor on its
corner. From that box, onion-skin ghosts climb an arc to the solid hero at the apex, upper right, looking at the viewer;
the three teammates stand bottom-right, looking up at it. Bottom-left:
"45s brand film" and the GitHub tag with the handle. The centre stays open enough for LinkedIn's play button.

FORM: Animator's spacing chart, carried by the onion skins and the first/last time stamps alone, position 1 of 1 on an
extension. seed: none (no roll):
new-work, "Extend an existing surface … No concept tournament", and "Never run the script for a local extension or a
precisely specified narrow request".

ADAPTATIONS (cited): the headline runs in three lines at 172 px so the arc has a clear lane and the title reads at
390 px; "45s brand film" sits under the headline so the footer belongs to the GitHub tag. The solid hero sits at the end
of the arc, past the apex: in an onion-skin chart the solid frame is the present and the ghosts are the past.

USER DECISION (after review): no drawn lines or dots. The arc line and its ticks, the stage-light ring, the dotted path
to the team and the background dust were removed at the user's request ("these lines are looking weird... remove these
lines and dots"). Do not reintroduce them.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and
every shipping raster carrying its provenance
